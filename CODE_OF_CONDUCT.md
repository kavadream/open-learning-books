# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific, and open to correction. Critique ideas and behavior without personal attacks. Welcome contributors across languages, cultures, and experience levels. Explain decisions and give people a practical way to improve their contributions.

Do not harass, discriminate, impersonate, expose private information, spam, or misrepresent learning results. Keep issue discussions related to the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive public content and restrict repeated misconduct. Report sensitive concerns through GitHub's private reporting tools when available; do not publish credentials or personal evidence in an issue.
